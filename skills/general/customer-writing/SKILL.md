# Customer Writing

Use this method only when explicitly requested by the user or selected by Good
Writing. An ordinary matching request goes through that router.

Help the recipient understand the situation and act. Use the reader, purpose,
facts and constraints from Good Writing, or infer them from a direct request.
Ask only about a gap that changes the message. Do not route back to Good Writing.

**Always apply `unslop`**, including during direct use. Load it once through the
host's skill catalog and returned path or read interface unless already loaded.
If unavailable, name the missing dependency and leave the language review open;
continue independent preparation without claiming the full method was applied.

## Make the message useful

Lead with the point, decision or request. Give the relevant reason, necessary
conditions and concrete next action. Include known dates, attachments, amounts
and responsible people when needed. Do not invent a deadline, commitment,
recipient preference or permission to send. Use a placeholder only for a
necessary detail that cannot safely be inferred.

Match the relationship and established tone. Preserve the intended du/Sie form
in German and the recipient's register in English, with an appropriate greeting
and sign-off. Routine coordination needs neither a formal brief nor a sales pitch.

Keep internal agent activity, tool names and checks out of external messages
unless the recipient needs them. Translate a finding into its practical effect.
Keep a condition beside the offer or statement it limits.

## Shape it for the channel

Group related questions or actions together. Use short paragraphs, bullets or
numbered steps when they make the response easier; emphasize the decision or
deadline sparingly. Match the actual email or chat format. Use plain text where
rich text is unavailable, and avoid raw Markdown syntax in the delivered message.
Longer correspondence can use meaningful topic headings; a short reply needs none.

For a process, relationship or handoff that benefits from a graphic, load
`editorial-svg-diagrams` with the verified facts, recipient and output needs. It owns
editable source, export and visual checks. If unavailable, disclose the limit
and use a clear supported form; an explicitly requested graphic remains open.

For mixed correspondence and sales arguments, apply the commercial brief selected
by Good Writing to that part. Keep this method focused on the relationship and
channel; specialist selection belongs to the router or an explicit user choice.

## Check and deliver

Check the recipient can find the point, necessary conditions and next action;
verify names, dates, attachments and commitments against the supplied facts.
Return the finished draft. Use the channel's tools only for an authorized write
or send, then verify that effect at its owner. Drafting alone authorizes neither.
